A damper is a timing device.

      
The part that decides how your car drives is the part you never see. So open one up.

The spring holds the car up.
So what controls it?

        
      

      

        
The damper controls the timing.
When load arrives, how long it stays, and how quickly it leaves.

        
      

      

        
This is the valving.
The single biggest differentiator between a mediocre damper and a brilliant one.

        
      

      

        
Now see it move.

        

        
At rest
Low-speed compression deals with body movement. High-speed compression deals with sharp inputs from the surface.

        
The speeds are damper shaft speeds, not car speeds. Illustration, not a simulation.

        
      

      

        
Adjusters cannot rescue bad valving.
So we start from your chassis, your tyres and your use, not a part number.
